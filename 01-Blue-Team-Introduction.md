# Blue Team Introduction

**TryHackMe SOC Level 1 — Module 1**

**Status:** ✅ Completed

## Overview

This module introduced the role of the **Blue Team** and the **Security Operations Centre (SOC)**, with a focus on the responsibilities of a Junior Security Analyst and common attack vectors targeting people and systems.

## Topics Covered

### Junior Security Analyst

I explored the role of a Junior Security Analyst and the types of activities performed during a typical day within a security operations environment.

This introduced the responsibilities and workflows involved in working within a SOC.

### SOC Role in the Blue Team

I learned about different security roles within a Blue Team and how a SOC analyst can progress through different levels of responsibility, starting with the **Level 1 (L1) analyst** role.

A key takeaway was understanding how SOC analysts contribute to the wider defensive security function by monitoring, analysing and responding to security events.

### Humans as Attack Vectors

I explored how people can become targets or entry points for cyber attacks.

This highlighted the importance of understanding human-focused threats and the role of security teams in identifying and defending against attacks that target users.

### Systems as Attack Vectors

I learned how attackers can exploit **vulnerable or misconfigured systems** and the importance of securing systems against these weaknesses.

This introduced the relationship between system vulnerabilities, attacker activity and defensive security measures.

## Key Takeaways

* A SOC forms a central part of an organisation's defensive security operations.
* Junior Security Analysts play an important role in monitoring and responding to security events.
* Cyber attacks can target both **people and systems**.
* Human behaviour can create opportunities for attackers, making user-focused security an important defensive consideration.
* Vulnerable or misconfigured systems can provide attackers with opportunities to compromise an environment.
* Effective Blue Team operations require understanding both the **attack surface** and the methods used to defend it.

## Skills Developed

* Understanding the purpose and function of a SOC
* Understanding the role of a Junior Security Analyst
* Identifying people and systems as potential attack vectors
* Developing a defensive security mindset
* Understanding the role of Blue Team operations in cybersecurity

## Reflection

This module provided the foundation for my SOC Level 1 learning by introducing the responsibilities of a SOC analyst and the different ways an organisation can be targeted. It also helped me understand why both users and technical systems need to be considered when defending an environment.
